Zoo360
On a trip to the Philadelphia Zoo, in Pennsylvania, kids got a surprise. Directly in front of them was a tiger. But the kids were not scared. They laughed because the tiger was in a special enclosed trail.
The trail is part of Zoo360. This group of trails gives animals plenty of room to move around, and is meant to feel like a natural environment. There are trees, plants, and lots of light, and the trail has changed the way humans and animals experience the Philadelphia Zoo.
Still, some people are wondering if we should still have zoos at all. Studies show that many animals are smarter than we once thought, so they might suffer when they are removed from the wild.
Others say zoos are important places for people to learn about animals and conservation. Many experts say zoos need to change if they’re going to last, and the Philadelphia Zoo is a good model for that.
